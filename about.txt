--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
About
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CADaver is an interactive CAD inspection, 3D mesh visualization, and volumetric
spatial analysis platform built with C++20 and Vulkan.

Link: https://github.com/Liech/CADaver
Version: 0.2dev

License: MIT
 * 0.1 used OCCT and was released under LGPL-2.1
 * 0.2 and ascending do not use OCCT and are released under MIT
